# The Fold brand assets

Mark: Euclid I.47 (squares on the sides of a right triangle). Wordmark: IBM Plex Mono Medium, lowercase, tracking -0.04em, "the" muted, "fold" full ink.

## Colour
| role | light | dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink | #111113 | #ecebf0 |
| violet | #6d28d9 | #a78bfa |
| triangle | #d9d4e8 | #3a3550 |
| "the" | #6b6875 | #9b9aa5 |
| background | #ffffff | #0f0f12 |

## Files
- mark/ square mark: light, dark, mono black, mono white (SVG) + 512 PNG
- wordmark/ text only, light and dark (font embedded)
- lockup/ horizontal and stacked, light and dark (font embedded); PNG at 4x
- app-icon/ 1024 rounded square, dark and light SVG; dark PNG at 1024, 512, 180
- favicon/ SVG, 32 and 16 PNG

## Use
- Clear space around the lockup: the height of the violet square's side.
- Minimum size: mark 16px, horizontal lockup 24px tall.
- Violet stays in the mark. The wordmark is ink and gray only.
