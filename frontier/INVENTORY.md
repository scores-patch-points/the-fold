- **khora `pipeline-atoms` / `stage-atoms-scope`**: both branches are ancestors of main (`git rev-list --count main..origin/<b>` = 0). They hold no unmerged work.
- **The-fold's vendored khora** (`vendor/khora/VENDOR-KHORA.json`) is pinned at `e1e1dac`, **204 commits behind** khora main. frontier/ does not use it; it goes through the contract instead.
- **Unmerged, not on main (cited, not used)**: khora branch `wilson-trail-fixes` (worktree `.wilson-wt/khora`, uncommitted at the time of reading) fixes two stigmergy defects: dead trails walked before scouts, and scouts stopping once every route has a deposit.
